Voltrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · App: Voltrail (com.voltrail.game) · Developer: Sathiyagan

Voltrail is an offline puzzle game for Android made by Sathiyagan ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

In short: Voltrail does not collect any personal information, does not send anything over the internet and does not share anything with anyone.

What the game saves

Your progress (levels, stars, settings and language) is saved only on your own device, inside the game's private storage. It never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the game, that data is deleted with it.

The optional "Backup progress" feature turns your progress into a text code on your device. You decide where to copy or paste that code. We never receive it.

Internet, ads and tracking

Voltrail does not connect to the internet. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no sign-in.

Permissions

The game asks for one Android permission: vibration, used for small haptic taps while you play. You can switch vibration off in the game's Options.

Purchases

Everything in the in-game shop is paid for with stars you earn by playing. There are no real-money purchases.

Links to other services

The "Rate Voltrail" button opens the Google Play Store app. Google's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

Voltrail is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever adds a feature that changes any of this, such as online play, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
